FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How soon can you come out?
For urgent problems we offer same-day appointments whenever possible, and we'll always give you a clear arrival window when you book. Standard jobs are scheduled at a time that suits you — and we show up on time.
Do I need to be home for the appointment?
Yes, an adult (18+) must be present for all service appointments. This is for insurance and safety reasons. If you need to reschedule, give us at least 2 hours notice and there's no cancellation fee.
Are your technicians licensed and insured?
Absolutely. Every Smiley Friendly Plumber technician holds a current plumbing license, carries full liability insurance, and passes a thorough background check before joining our team. We never subcontract to unvetted workers.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, checks, and bank transfers. Payment is due upon job completion. For larger projects, we offer a 50/50 split — 50% upfront, 50% when done.
What does the 1-year warranty cover?
Our labor warranty covers any defect in workmanship for 12 months from the date of service. If something we installed or repaired fails due to our work, we return and fix it at zero cost. Parts warranties vary by manufacturer, typically 90 days to 1 year.
